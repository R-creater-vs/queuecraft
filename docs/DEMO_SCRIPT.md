# QUEUECRAFT — 60-Second Recruiter & Technical Demo Script

## Hook
“The average wait improved, but these visitors paid for that improvement. Follow the exact scheduling decisions.”

---

## The 60-Second Walkthrough

### 0:00 - 0:15: The Service Theatre (The Illusion of the Average)
- **Visual:** Open **Service Theatre**.
- **Narrative:**
  > "Every workforce management software shows an average wait time slider. If you concentrate staff during the morning rush, average wait drops. But under a fixed staff-hour budget, you cannot create labor out of thin air. Moving staff creates winners and losers. QUEUECRAFT traces every single visitor's journey through the queue on this discrete-event time rail."

### 0:15 - 0:35: The Signature Ledger (Who Waited Longer?)
- **Visual:** Click **Who Waited Longer?**. Point to the KPI cards and the Victim Rate.
- **Narrative:**
  > "Here is our signature contribution: the **Matched Wait-Transfer Ledger**. We simulate the candidate schedule against the exact same visitor arrival stream. Notice the breakdown: while morning visitors saved 233 minutes, afternoon visitors were hit with **+14 minutes of extra wait**. The ledger satisfies an exact mathematical conservation law: $\sum \Delta w = \sum \text{Harm} - \sum \text{Benefit}$."

### 0:35 - 0:50: The Budget Frontier & Finite Constraints
- **Visual:** Click **Budget Frontier**.
- **Narrative:**
  > "On the **Budget Frontier**, every candidate schedule has the exact same 720 scheduled staff-minutes. We can evaluate multiobjective constraints—such as ensuring no visitor waits more than 5 extra minutes—while tracking shift-ending overtime separately so schedules cannot cheat the budget."

### 0:50 - 1:00: Performance & Closing
- **Visual:** Click **Scenario Notebook**. Point to the CLI test command and export button.
- **Narrative:**
  > "The discrete-event simulation engine processes 10,000 visitors in 22 milliseconds, 100% offline with zero external dependencies."
